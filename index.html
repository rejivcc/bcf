About us

Ordinary people, gathered around an extraordinary story.

We're a Bendigo-based church family that's been meeting for over twenty years. No two people here look, vote, or think exactly alike — and we think that's exactly how it should be. What holds us together is Jesus, and a shared conviction that faith should shape ordinary Tuesdays as much as Sunday mornings.

Whether you grew up in church, walked away from one, or have never set foot in one, you're welcome to come as you are and ask anything.

  • Welcome firstNobody is asked to have it figured out before they belong.
  • Scripture, honestly taughtWe teach the Bible in plain language, questions included.
  • Faith with our sleeves rolled upWe show up for our neighbours through food relief, aged care visits, and local partnerships.
Leadership

Meet our pastor

Pastor Reji Samuel

Lead Pastor

Reji has led the fellowship since 2022 alongside his wife, Manju. He's known around Bendigo as much for his Saturday morning parkrun as his Sunday teaching, and he'd tell you the two aren't unrelated — showing up consistently is most of what faith and fitness have in common.
